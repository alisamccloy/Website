I Promise

I Promise is an apocalyptic-drama short film about a young woman who remains isolated in her small room as she fears the world outside to be too dangerous. For comfort and sanity, she clings to the memory of a loved one's promise before everything changed. The film's premise is centred around a feeling of isolation and a sense of entrapment, emphasised through the governmental control over the young woman's life.
